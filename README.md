# Resources for Chemistry

**[Chemical Principles I (CHEM 1211K)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-1211k) at Georgia Tech.** A first-semester course in general chemistry. Of note is that the entirety of chemical thermodynamics typically covered across the two-semester general chemistry sequence is covered in CHEM 1211K at Georgia Tech. Topics covered include models and representations of atoms, ions, and molecules, the mole, models of aqueous solutions, precipitation reactions, stoichiometry, thermochemistry, gases, chemical thermodynamics, the quantum model of the atom, periodic trends, Lewis structures, molecular geometry, theories of covalent bonding, and intermolecular forces.

**[Chemical Principles II (CHEM 1211K)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-1212k) at Georgia Tech.** A second-semester course in general chemistry. Topics covered include chemical kinetics, chemical equilibrium, the connection between equilibrium and thermodynamics, acids and bases, aqueous equilibria, coordination chemistry, and main-group chemistry as explained by the Lewis theory of acids and bases.

**[General Chemistry for Engineers (CHEM 1310)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-1310) at Georgia Tech.** A one-semester survey of general chemistry for engineering majors needing only a smattering of chemistry. Topics covered include models and representations of atoms, ions, and molecules, stoichiometry, thermochemistry, gases, chemical thermodynamics, the quantum model of the atom, Lewis structures, molecular geometry, chemical kinetics, chemical equilibrium, and electrochemistry.

**[Organic Chemistry I (CHEM 2311)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-2311) at Georgia Tech.** A first-semester course in organic chemistry. Topics covered include foundations, representing organic molecules, stereochemistry, alkanes and cycloalkanes (including conformational analysis), essentials of organic reaction mechanisms, reactions of alkyl halides and pseudohalides, reactions of alkenes and alkynes, and radical reactions.

**[Organic Chemistry II (CHEM 2312)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-2312) at Georgia Tech.** A second-semester course in organic chemistry, with a focus on biochemical applications. Topics covered include pericyclic reactions, structures and reactions of aromatic and heteroaromatic compounds, structures and properties of carbonyl compounds, nucleophilic additions to the carbonyl carbon, reactions at the α-carbon of carbonyl compounds, carbonyl condensation reactions, carbohydrates, amino acids, peptides and proteins, and more.

**[Molecular Photochemistry (CHEM 4801 MP)](https://github.com/mevans86/molecular-photochemistry) at Georgia Tech.** A five-week "mini-mester" course covering the foundations of photochemistry of small molecules. A solid understanding of quantum chemistry is needed to get the most from the course. Topics covered include the structures and dynamics of molecular excited states, photophysical transitions, photochemical energy transfer and electron transfer, experimental methods in photochemistry, and photochemical reactions of carbonyl compounds and alkenes.
