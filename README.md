# Resources for General Chemistry

**[Chemical Principles I (CHEM 1211K)]([url](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-1211k)) at Georgia Tech.** A first-semester course in general chemistry. Of note is that the entirety of chemical thermodynamics (including enthalpy, entropy, and free energy) typically covered across the two-semester general chemistry sequence is covered in CHEM 1211K at Georgia Tech.

**[Chemical Principles II (CHEM 1211K)]([url](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-1212k)) at Georgia Tech.** A second-semester course in general chemistry. Topics covered include chemical kinetics, chemical equilibrium, the connection between equilibrium and thermodynamics, acids and bases, aqueous equilibria, coordination chemistry, and main-group chemistry as explained by the Lewis theory of acids and bases.

**[Organic Chemistry I (CHEM 2311)](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-2311) at Georgia Tech.** A first-semester course in organic chemistry. Topics covered include foundations, representing organic molecules, stereochemistry, alkanes and cycloalkanes (including conformational analysis), essentials of organic reaction mechanisms, reactions of alkyl halides and pseudohalides, reactions of alkenes and alkynes, and radical reactions.

**[Organic Chemistry II (CHEM 2312)]([url](https://github.com/mevans86/chem-2312)) at Georgia Tech.** A second-semester course in organic chemistry, with a focus on biochemical applications. Topics covered include pericyclic reactions, structures and reactions of aromatic and heteroaromatic compounds, structures and properties of carbonyl compounds, nucleophilic additions to the carbonyl carbon, reactions at the α-carbon of carbonyl compounds, carbonyl condensation reactions, carbohydrates, amino acids, peptides and proteins, and more.
